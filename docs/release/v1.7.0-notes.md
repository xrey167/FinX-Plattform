# Release Notes — v1.7.0

> **Status: pending tag.** Bundles the FinX Partner program — 6 waves, PRs
> #437–#442 — merged to `main` after the v1.6.0 tag.

The **FinX Partner release.** v1.4.0–v1.6.0 built the substrate: the MCP
financial-data server, the self-hosted warehouse, the knowledge/learning system,
and total OpenBB command parity. v1.7.0 ties them into one **autonomous,
learning, human-in-the-loop partner** — a single conversational front door that
answers from data *and* knowledge, proactively surfaces what changed, gets
better with use, and acts autonomously within gates while keeping you in control
through an after-the-fact audit/undo surface.

This is a **MINOR** release: all changes are backward-compatible additions — a
new `tdw-partner` crate and thin adapters. No protocol, persistence-schema, or
operator-contract breaks.

## What the FinX Partner does

A new `tdw-partner` crate provides **one `PartnerCore`** that every surface
shares; the MCP tools, the OpenBB Workspace copilot, and the CLI are thin
adapters over it (no per-surface logic).

### One front door — "ask FinX anything" (W2)
`tdw.partner.ask` (MCP), the Workspace copilot, and `tdw partner ask` (CLI) all
route a natural-language question through `PartnerCore::turn`: it resolves the
question to data routes (across the 195 Fetch routes) and/or knowledge
(`tdw.kg.*`), **extracts the parameters from your utterance**, fetches with
provenance, grounds an answer, and writes the turn back as episodic memory +
candidate findings. The Workspace copilot is now powered by the same core — one
seam (`agent_bridge`) lights up all three surfaces.

### Proactive — briefs + nudges (W3)
A unified proactive stream: a scheduled **daily brief** (via `tdw-cron`) and
event-driven **nudges** (off the knowledge feed) that fold together alerts,
watchlists, thesis health, open questions, contradictions, and staleness — what
changed, what the partner noticed, what needs you. Dismissals feed learning.

### Learning loop — gets better with use (W4)
Every turn reads the **gated** runtime (promoted lessons, induced rules, tuned
parameters, learned route preferences) so the partner's behavior improves only
through eval/B9-gated promotions — never silent mutation. A walk-forward eval
harness measures rising usefulness.

### Audit-only autonomy — act within gates, review after (W5–W6)
Per the design directive, the partner is **fully autonomous within its
eval/trust gates** and you review **after the fact**: an `audit` feed projects
every action with its `why`, and a one-gesture **`undo`** performs *real*
reversal on the already-reversible governed-forgetting / cold-plane machinery
(knowledge writes retired, parameter tunes restored, rule promotions demoted) —
`correct` = undo + feedback that trains the system. Only low-confidence,
high-impact, or irreversible actions escalate to wait-for-human. There is **no
pre-approval inbox** — reversibility plus the eval gates are the safety net.

### Cohesion & onboarding (W6)
An anti-duplication test enforces that the adapters carry no core logic; a guided
**zero-to-partner** first run gets you from nothing to the full ask→brief→audit
loop quickly; the default tool surface leads with the few high-value verbs
(`ask`/`brief`/`audit`) with the rest progressively discoverable.

## Upgrade notes

- **No breaking changes.** The `tdw-partner` crate and its adapters are additive;
  existing MCP/REST/CLI/Workspace contracts are unchanged.
- **Autonomy is audit-only by default**: the partner acts within its gates and
  records every action to the audit feed; review and `undo`/`correct` after the
  fact. Genuinely high-impact/irreversible actions still wait for you.
